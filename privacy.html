Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Alpha Tribe Menswear Private Limited

1. Commitment to Privacy

At Alpha Tribe Menswear Private Limited ("Alpha Tribe", "we", "us", or "our"), safeguarding your confidentiality is a sacred matter of honor. We collect only information necessary to deliver garments with surgical precision and ensure an immaculate client experience.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our storefront, we collect:

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, telephone number, shipping address, and email address.
  • Order Details: Products purchased, fit sizes selected, transaction values, and payment confirmation tokens.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and operating system collected anonymously for performance optimization.

3. Payment Security & PCI-DSS Compliance

We do never store your Credit/Debit Card numbers, CVVs, or Net Banking credentials on our local servers. All digital payments (UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Net Banking) are tokenized and processed through RBI-approved Indian payment aggregators protected by 256-bit SSL encryption.

4. How We Utilize Your Data

Your details are utilized strictly for:

  • Fulfilling orders and coordinating delivery with verified courier partners (Bluedart, Delhivery).
  • Dispatching dispatch notifications, real-time tracking links, and digital GST invoices via SMS and email.
  • Facilitating 7-day complimentary doorstep size exchanges.
  • Notifying members of the private VIP Clan regarding limited capsule drops (only upon opt-in consent).

5. Non-Disclosure & Third Parties

Alpha Tribe does never sell, barter, or lease customer data to third-party marketing brokers. Data is shared exclusively with certified logistical couriers solely for doorstep fulfillment.
