Our Heritage & Cut

Not Just Clothing.
A Quiet Dominance.

Most international menswear is cut for European torso proportions—either too tight across the shoulders or ballooning around the waist.

Alpha Tribe re-engineered the pattern from the ground up: broader shoulder spans, refined armholes that do not ride up, and a subtle taper down the ribcage. Tailored for comfort in India’s climate, built for authority anywhere in the world.
