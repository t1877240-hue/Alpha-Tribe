Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our precision fits, logistics, and doorstep service.

✦ Sizing & The Alpha Fit

How does the Alpha Tribe fit differ from standard slim fit? +
Standard Western slim fits constrict broad Indian shoulders, pull across the chest, or create unflattering bunching around the belt. Alpha Tribe features an engineered Athletic Taper: generous chest room, an elevated armhole radius that prevents untucking, and a clean taper toward the waist.
What if my garment doesn’t fit perfectly? +
We provide 100% complimentary doorstep size exchanges. Within 7 days of delivery, visit our Returns & Exchanges Portal, enter your order number, and our courier will swap your size directly at your door.
Are sizes in inches or centimeters? +
Our sizing charts display garment dimensions in inches. For tops (shirts, polos, tees), size S corresponds to 38, M to 40, L to 42, XL to 44, and XXL to 46. Denim jeans correspond directly to true waist measurement (30 to 38 inches).

✦ Pan-India Shipping & Delivery

How fast will my order arrive? +
All orders are dispatched from our central Mumbai logistics hub within 24 hours. Metro deliveries (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune) arrive within 2–3 business days via Bluedart Air. Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities take 3–5 business days.
Is Cash on Delivery (COD) supported? +
Yes, Cash on Delivery is supported across 19,000+ Indian PIN codes at zero extra charge. You may pay with cash or scan the courier’s UPI QR code directly at your doorstep.
What are the shipping charges? +
Shipping is completely FREE on all orders of ₹1,999 or more. For smaller orders under ₹1,999, a nominal flat express shipping fee of ₹150 applies.

✦ 7-Day Returns & Exchanges

What is your return & exchange window? +
You have 7 full days from the date of delivery to initiate an exchange or return. Garments must be in their original unwashed condition with tags attached.
How are refunds processed? +
Once our courier picks up the garment, your refund is initiated immediately. For prepaid orders (UPI/Card), funds credit back to your original payment method in 24–48 hours. For COD orders, we transfer instantly to your verified UPI ID or bank account.

✦ Fabric Care & Integrity

Will Alpha Tribe garments shrink after washing? +
No. All our fabrics undergo commercial pre-shrinking and enzyme softening washes before cutting. Follow our cold-wash care guidelines on the label and your garments will preserve their tailored silhouette for years.
How do I care for raw selvedge denim? +
For raw denim purists, wear the jeans regularly for 4–6 months before the first wash to allow natural honeycombing and whiskering. When washing, turn inside out, soak in cold water with mild dark detergent, and hang dry away from direct sun.
